AISTATS 2027 sits where machine learning meets statistics. In AI, conferences like this are full, blind-reviewed publications, not just talks.

AISTATS is the International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics. CORE rank A in the 2017 to 2023 rankings; the 2025 edition accepted 583 of 1,861 submissions, about 31 percent. Proceedings appear in PMLR, the series linked to the Journal of Machine Learning Research. Our manuscript is submitted and under review; acceptance is not claimed. In AI, top conferences play the role that journals play in many engineering fields.
