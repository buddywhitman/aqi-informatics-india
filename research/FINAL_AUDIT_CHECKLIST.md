# Research Branch Final Audit Checklist

## Reproducibility
- All newly claimed numeric findings have a committed script and/or committed result summary.
- GitHub Actions executes the main synthetic studies.
- Negative results are retained.
- Linear-operator "validation" was corrected to an algebraic identity.
- Submission branches/materials were not modified by this research program.

## Claim discipline
- Synthetic oracle claims are labeled synthetic/oracle.
- Observational sensor results are not treated as causal ground truth.
- Broad prior-art claims have been downgraded where collisions were found.
- Representation orthogonality remains future methodology, not a completed estimator.
- Task-aware calibration is not claimed as a new general concept.
- Strongly identified targets under weak global identification are not claimed as new.

## Surviving strongest findings
1. Phantom causal resolution.
2. Self-canceling proxy reliability diagnostics.
3. Task/direction-relative representation damage in latent causal geometry.
4. Conservative posterior-moment/sensitivity/validation framework.
5. Dual adjustment/target resolution as a useful synthesis.

## Critical negatives
- Original task-conditioning hump was nuisance leakage.
- D does not robustly outperform entropy in zoo.
- Temperature scaling improves ECE without improving D-AUC.
- Generic geometry disagreement is near chance.
- Simple task-weighted Brier gives only modest broad-sweep gain.
- Moment completion is misspecification-sensitive.
- lambda_min can be both optimistic and irrelevant/pessimistic.
- Cross-fitting does not remove systematic representation bias.
- Partial correction can fail if wrong directions are corrected.
- Local operator fails under strong curvature/weak information.
- Oracle fidelity does not establish structural validity.
- Delhi pre-treatment placebos fail despite favorable geometry.
- Mumbai is materially frozen-sensor sensitive.

## Remaining limitations that cannot be honestly closed from current repository
- No ground-truth latent states for real cities.
- No randomized/interventional real-data causal ground truth.
- Aggregate zoo tables lack per-observation posterior x causal-leverage arrays.
- No labeled-state validation subset for operational b_gamma bounds.
- No fully operational representation-orthogonal estimator.
- No complete rigorous dependent-data theorem for the unified expansion.
- Focused novelty audit is strong but cannot prove absence of all prior art.

## Stop criterion
Further synthetic examples should be added only if they can falsify/change one of the surviving contributions. Otherwise effort should move to theorem proof, new validation data, or a new paper built around the surviving package.
